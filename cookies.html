01What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. Local storage (localStorage) is a similar technology that lets a site remember information in your browser. This policy explains which ones the Site uses, in line with the GDPR, the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, the UK PECR and other applicable laws.

02What we use

The Site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not build browsing profiles. It only uses functional or preference items that are strictly necessary for features you request. They therefore do not require consent (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive / Reg. 6 PECR), but we disclose them for transparency:

NamePurposeTypeCategoryDuration
aria-langRemembers the language you chose (EN / ES / PT).First-party · local storageFunctional / preferencesUntil you clear it

This data stays in your browser: it is not sent to our servers or to third parties.

03Third-party services

To load some resources, your browser connects to third-party servers. These services do not set tracking cookies from the Site, but they technically receive your IP address:

ProviderUse
Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC)Delivers typefaces. When the page loads, your browser connects to Google servers and sends them your IP address and technical browser data.
jsDelivr (Prospect One / Cloudflare / Fastly)Content delivery network (CDN) for Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons. Receives your IP address to deliver the files.

More information in our Privacy Policy.

04How to manage or delete this data

You can view, block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

If you delete them, the Site will keep working but will forget your saved preferences.

05If we add analytics or advertising

If we add analytics tools, advertising pixels or embedded content that sets non-essential cookies (for example Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or YouTube videos), we will ask for your prior consent through a banner that lets you accept, reject or customise them just as easily, and we will update this policy before enabling them. We also honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.
